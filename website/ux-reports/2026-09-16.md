# UX review 2026-09-16

Question: what does a first-time visitor to deadlock-api.com try to do, where do they get stuck, and what one navigation change fixes the most of it.

Context: two changes from the previous runs are live behind PostHog experiments and split traffic 50/50: `exp-landing-quick-links` (quick-link row on `/`) and `exp-hero-row-link` (hero image plus name on `/heroes` as one visibly styled link). The synthetic sessions below block PostHog, so they saw the control variant of both.

## 2. Qualitative: synthetic first-time users

Fresh isolated headless Chrome per persona, PostHog requests aborted, desktop 1440x900 unless stated. Both experiments therefore rendered their control arm.

### P1 New player: which hero has the best win rate right now

1. Landing. First screen: title, sponsor logo, three badges, intro paragraph, patron box. "Hero Analytics" card top edge at 873px of a 900px fold. No quick-link row (control arm).
2. Click sidebar **Heroes**. `/heroes` table sorted by win rate, Celeste 55.1% on row 1.

Goal reached in 1 click. Default filter "Rank: Phantom 1+" is not something a new player would understand, same as last run.

### P2 Plays Haze: what items to build this patch

1. Click sidebar **Heroes**.
2. No search box on the page. Haze is row 25 of 38, at 1770px, two screens down. Hero name renders as plain white text with no underline (underline only on hover), and the row's only visible control is the (i) "Details" button, which opens a stats popover rather than the hero page.
3. Click **Haze** (the name, once hovered). `/heroes/haze` opens with "Best Haze Items" at 331px, then "Haze Skill Order", win rate over time, by rank, by duration, matchups.

Goal reached in 2 clicks plus a scroll. Dead ends: no way to jump to a hero by name; the link affordance is invisible at rest (the running `exp-hero-row-link` test targets the second point).

### P3 Developer: API docs and how to fetch match data

1. Landing. Nothing in the first screen says "API" or "docs". The sidebar pill **Game Data** (Services group, at 767px) is the only entry; the "Developer Services" section is at 1450px.
2. Click **Game Data**. Lands on `api.deadlock-api.com/docs` (Scalar). Sidebar lists **Matches** as the second group with an expand button.

Goal reached in 2 clicks. The quick-links experiment adds an "API Docs" button to the test arm.

### P4 Streamer: overlay or chat command tooling

1. Click sidebar **Stream Kit**. Page subtitle: "Build chat commands and OBS widgets for your Deadlock stream". Content: Steam ID3 input, "Sign in with Steam", region radio. Nothing shows which commands or widgets exist before connecting.

Goal reached in 1 click, but the visitor cannot evaluate the tool without an account. Content finding, unchanged from last run.

### P5 Mobile (390x844): hero win rates within two taps

1. Landing first screen: hamburger, title, sponsor, badges, intro paragraph, top of the patron box. Links visible: sponsor, Open Source, Open Data, Free to Use. "Hero Analytics" card at 1261px.
2. Tap **Open menu**. Drawer with 17 links; Heroes is the fifth, at 275px.
3. Tap **Heroes**. `/heroes` renders the win rate table under the filter block; Celeste at 693px, visible on the first screen.

Goal reached in exactly 2 taps, via the hamburger only. Same as last run; the quick-links experiment targets this.

## 3. Ranked findings

Ranking = share of first-time sessions affected x severity. Every finding lists the PostHog table and persona that support it.

1. **Hero pages are hidden on `/heroes`.** 488 sessions (35% of all) view `/heroes`, 340 interact with it, 71 reach a hero page (1.4). 81% of the 330 sessions landing there see nothing else (1.6). Synthetic: name link has no rest styling and the (i) button in the "Details" column opens a popover, not the page (P2). Severity high: the hero page holds the best items and skill order, the second most common goal. **Already under test** by `exp-hero-row-link` (early: 16.4% vs 11.5%). Not acted on again here; let the experiment run.
2. **No way to find a specific hero on `/heroes`.** The table is 38 rows sorted by win rate with no name filter; a mid-table hero sits two screens down on desktop and further on mobile (P2). PostHog: 9 sessions clicked the "Hero" header to sort by name (1.4 follow-up), the only workaround the page offers. Affects every "I play hero X" visitor among the 488 `/heroes` sessions. Severity medium. Not covered by either experiment. **Acted on.**
3. **Landing page shows no stats link in the first screen** on desktop (card at 873px) or mobile (1261px) (P1, P5). PostHog agrees: only 7 first clicks on `/` hit the analytics cards vs 41 on the sidebar "Heroes" link (1.2). Already under test by `exp-landing-quick-links`. Not acted on.
4. **Stream Kit shows nothing before Steam sign-in** (P4). 12 landing sessions, 48 pageviews. Low share. Content change, out of scope.
5. **Developer entry is a small sidebar pill** (P3). 5 first clicks on "Game Data" from `/` (1.2). Low severity, reached in 1 click. Folded into the quick-links test.

PostHog-only, no synthetic reproduction, reported not acted on: 39 sessions rage-click on `/deadlockdle/guess-sound` (1.5).

Not findings, by design: patron-only player profiles and tracker, absence of public player search. For the record, "Player Tracker" is the most common first click from `/` (84 sessions, 1.2) and 94 sessions go `/` then `/tracker`; 35 of those end there, 39 continue to `/games`, 17 to `/patron`.

## 4. Recommended change (implemented on `ux/hero-name-filter`)

Add a "Find a hero…" search input on the Overall Stats tab of `/heroes`, left of the "Group by Type" toggle. Typing filters the table rows by hero name (case-insensitive substring) while keeping each hero's rank number, so "haz" shows "25 Haze" and nothing else. Works in the grouped view too. Local state only, no URL param, no API change.

Files: `src/routes/heroes.index.tsx` (input and state), `src/components/heroes-page/HeroStatsTable.tsx` (`nameQuery` prop and row filter).

Verified in dev at 1440x900 and 390x844: typing "haz" leaves one row, "Haze #25".

## 5. What to measure after it ships

Compare 14 days after deploy against this report, per `$session_id`:

- Share of `/heroes` sessions with an `$autocapture` on the input (`aria-label` "Filter heroes by name", `elements_chain` contains it). This is the adoption number. If under 3% of `/heroes` sessions after 500 sessions, the box is not worth its space; revert.
- Share of `/heroes` sessions that reach `/heroes/<hero>` (1.4 follow-up, baseline 14.5% overall, 11.5% control / 16.4% test of `exp-hero-row-link`). The filter should lift both arms equally; check that the experiment's arm split stays balanced.
- Clicks on the "Hero" sort header per `/heroes` session (baseline 9 sessions in 14 days). Expect a fall.
- Guardrail: single-pageview sessions landing on `/heroes` must not rise above 81% + 5 points, and `exp-hero-row-link` guardrails (column-header sort clicks) must not fall.
